1. Endless Forms Most Beautiful

Endless Forms Most Beautiful is the eighth studio album by the Finnish symphonic metal band Nightwish. It was released on March 27, 2015 via Nuclear Blast. The album is the band's first featuring singer Floor Jansen and the first with Troy Donockley as a full-time member.

2. Manifest

Manifest is the sixth studio album by Swedish heavy metal band Amaranthe. It was released on October 2, 2020 via Nuclear Blast.

3. Circus of Doom

Circus of Doom is the sixth studio album by the Finnish metal band Battle Beast. It was released on January 21, 2022 via Nuclear Blast. Like its three predecessors, the album debuted at number one on the official Finnish Albums Chart.
